### Added
- **Camera-attached thermal switches ask which camera they are on, and bind it by serial** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP): the ZWO Dew Heater, the ToupTek thermal switch and the Player One thermal switch bound to a bare SDK index typed into a number box, so on a rig with an ASI290MM Mini at index 0 and an ASI2600MM Pro at index 1 the dew heater went to the camera with no heater and refused every connect with "Dew heater not supported". The web form replaces the number box with a camera dropdown that lists every attached camera with its index, puts the cameras with a heater or fan first, greys out the rest, labels a camera another switch already uses, preselects nothing for a new switch and will not save until a camera is picked. The ZWO and Player One switches save the picked camera's `serialNumber` and then bind only that camera, so a USB reorder cannot move the heater to another camera. The server also accepts `cameraIndex` `-1` (Auto: the one attached camera with the controls, refused with a list when there are none or several) for API clients; the form does not offer it. Saved switches keep the index they had.
- **`GET /management/v1/thermalcameras?vendor=<zwo|touptek|playerone>`** (AlpacaHTTP): the attached cameras for that vendor's thermal switch, each with `Index`, `Name`, `Serial` and `HasThermal`, read through the device catalog so AlpacaHTTP still includes no vendor header. ZWO opens each camera through the ref-counted SDK wrapper to read its controls and serial, Player One opens each cooled camera, ToupTek reads the enumeration flags only.

### Fixed
- **Thermal switch connect refusals name the camera to pick** (AlpacaCore): a switch bound to a camera without the controls now says which camera that is and which attached camera has them, for example "Camera index 0 is the ZWO ASI290MM Mini, which has no dew heater. Pick ZWO ASI2600MM Pro (index 1) in this switch's settings.", instead of "Dew heater not supported" or "Camera has no dew heater or fan (uncooled model?)". The text reaches clients such as NINA and Ara, and the web UI's `LastConnectError`. Error codes are unchanged.

### Added (tests)
- **Thermal camera choice, ToupTek and Player One binding cases, the endpoint, round trips and the web picker**: pure cases for the shared choice and refusal text; ToupTek and Player One fake-SDK cases for Auto, a pinned camera without controls, two heater cameras, serial binding and the listing's opens; `thermalcameras` routing cases and Auto/serial round-trip rows for all three switches; `node --test` cases for the picker model. `FakePlayerOneSDK` gains extra enumerated cameras and ref-counted opens per camera, as the real wrapper counts them.
