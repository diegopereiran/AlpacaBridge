### Changed
- **ToupTek SDK updated to 20260519** (ToupTek camera, focuser, filter-wheel and thermal-switch drivers, and the SVBONY SC571CC/SC715C rebadges they serve), issue #1131: the vendored `toupcamsdk.20260128` (59.30701) is replaced by `toupcamsdk.20260519` (60.31488) from ToupTek's SDK page, vendored as `inc/toupcam.h`, `linux/arm64/glibc/libtoupcam.so` and `linux/udev/99-toupcam.rules` only. The SDK's `samples/` (vendor demo programs that nothing in the build, packaging or tests uses) is no longer vendored, per the minimal-subset rule in `AlpacaCore/external/README.md`. The header only adds API (the zero-copy `Toupcam_PullImagePtr`/`WaitImagePtr`/`PushImagePtr`, `Toupcam_SnapV2`, TDI and frame-interval options) and raises the GigE GVCP retry maximum and the timeout default, minimum and maximum; every call the drivers make keeps its signature. The udev rules are unchanged. The SDK path moves in `debian/rules`, `build_and_run.sh`, `install_alpaca_service.sh`, the vendor `CMakeLists.txt`, `ci.yml` and `nightly-build.yml`; `SUPPORTED-DRIVERS.md` names the new SDK; the `scripts/dependency_sdk_versions.json` pin is rewritten. Re-validated on hardware with ConformU 4.5.3 (0 errors, 0 issues, 0 timing issues each): the GPCMOS01200KPF and ATR2600M cameras, the ATR2600M Thermal Switch, the AAF focuser and the AFW-M filter wheel; their reports under `AlpacaCore/conformu/ToupTek/` are replaced. These are the first camera runs on ConformU 4.5.3, so 4.5.3 is no longer provisional.
