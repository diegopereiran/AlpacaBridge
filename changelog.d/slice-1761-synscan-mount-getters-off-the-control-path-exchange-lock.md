### Changed

- **SynScan getters no longer wait on the handset**, SynScan telescope driver, issues #1761 and #1828. The connect handshake and the disconnect close no longer run under the driver's state lock: `Tracking`, `GuideRate`, `AtPark` and `IsPulseGuiding` answer within 20 ms while a connect is in flight at 200 ms per reply (`test_synscan_telescope_snapshot.cpp`, "Tracking and GuideRate do not wait for a connect that is in flight" and "each no-I/O getter alone is in budget while a connect is in flight"), and `set_connected(false)` returns within 5 s with a status tick, or two pulse bodies and a tick, mid-exchange at 1.2 s per reply (the bound holds up to about 1.18 s per reply when an AbortSlew is also in flight, and a silent handset at the default 5 s timeout is outside it) (same file, "set_connected(false) returns within 5 s ..."). The control-path helpers (site and time reads and writes, tracking mode, park position, the slewing re-read) send with the lock released; PulseGuide sends its mode read and rate command in one wire turn, and SyncToCoordinates is now serialised with Park, slews, MoveAxis and AbortSlew (`test_synscan_initiator_supersede.cpp`). The polled frame is served through `util::PolledState`. The fault text a latched link fault reports is now "SynScan mount communications compromised: no valid reply to the state poll (3 consecutive failures)" and no longer carries the failing exchange's reply; a good control-path read no longer clears a fault, the next good poll does. `Connected` reads false until the first frame is held at the end of a connect.
