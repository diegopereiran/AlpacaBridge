### Fixed
- **ZWO cameras keep their device number and UniqueID across restarts** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, issue #914): a saved camera entry now binds the camera by serial number instead of SDK enumeration order, so a reboot that swaps the order no longer hands a client the other camera. The first start records `serialNumber` and `cameraName` in the entry (`cameraIndex` and `cameraId` stay as hints, never a fallback to a camera with a different serial); a camera whose serial is gone fails connect with "ZWO camera serial <sn> not found". A body with no serial (ASI120MM Mini) binds by `cameraName`, skipping a serial another entry claims, and reports a stored `ZWO_UID_<16 hex>` UniqueID instead of `ZWO_<device number>`; existing entries get one on the first start after the upgrade. Pinned by the cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_camera_identity.cpp` and `ZWO Camera Driver - UniqueID follows the config entry, not the device number`.
