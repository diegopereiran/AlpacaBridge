### Fixed
- **ZWO cameras keep their device number and UniqueID across restarts** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, issue #914): a saved camera entry now binds the camera by serial number instead of SDK enumeration order, so a reboot that swaps the order no longer hands a client the other camera. The first start records `serialNumber` and `cameraName` in the entry (`cameraIndex` and `cameraId` stay as hints, never a fallback to a camera with a different serial); a camera whose serial is gone fails connect with "ZWO camera serial <sn> not found". A body with no serial (ASI120MM Mini) binds by `cameraName`, skipping a serial another entry claims, and reports a stored `ZWO_UID_<16 hex>` UniqueID instead of `ZWO_<device number>`; existing entries get one on the first start after the upgrade. Pinned by the cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_camera_identity.cpp` and `ZWO Camera Driver - UniqueID follows the config entry, not the device number`.
- **ZWO camera identity survives a web UI edit and refuses control bytes** (AlpacaHTTP, issue #914): editing a ZWO camera in the web form removes and re-adds it, so the form now resends the learned `serialNumber`, `cameraName` and `uniqueId` while `cameraIndex` and `cameraId` are unchanged (`AlpacaHTTP/web/app.js` `zwoCameraIdentityFields`). An entry with only an id or index hint no longer binds a camera whose serial another entry claims. `configuredevice` refuses a `serialNumber` that is not hex and a `cameraName` or `uniqueId` with control bytes as `InvalidValue`; a stored entry with such a value drops it with a warning. With a `cameraName` set, connect opens only cameras of that model to read serials, so other bodies are left alone.
- **ZWO camera edit identity no longer leaks into the next Add Device, and duplicates are refused** (AlpacaHTTP, issue #914): the form resends the learned identity only in edit mode and clears it on reset (`zwoCameraIdentityFields`, `resetDeviceForm` in `AlpacaHTTP/web/app.js`), and `configuredevice` refuses a `serialNumber` or `uniqueId` another ZWO camera entry holds as `InvalidValue`, pinned by the `zwo camera refuses a serial or uniqueId another entry holds (#914)` case in `AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_routing.cpp`.
