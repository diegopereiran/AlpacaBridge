### Fixed
- **SynScan: stopping a move no longer turns tracking back on after you turned it off at the handset** (SynScan, issue #1259): `AbortSlew`, `MoveAxis(0, 0)` and the end of an RA `PulseGuide` wrote the tracking mode cached at connect back to the handset, and `SlewToCoordinates` passed its "requires Tracking" check on that cached value. The driver now reads the handset's tracking mode when a motion starts (and before `AbortSlew` or a slew check with nothing running), so each stop restores the mode the handset held then, and sends nothing when tracking was off. Covered by "SynScan AbortSlew - does not turn tracking back on after a keypad change", "SynScan MoveAxis - stopping RA does not turn tracking back on after a keypad change", "SynScan PulseGuide - the end of an RA pulse does not turn tracking back on after a keypad change", "SynScan SlewToCoordinates - refuses after the keypad turned tracking off", "SynScan stops - restore the mode the handset held when the motion started" and "SynScan idle stops - still sent when the tracking read times out".
